This educational guide gathers public information about Amarilo, one of Colombia's largest homebuilders. Explore its VIS and No VIS housing projects, its "Academia de Soñadores" financial education program and its focus on sustainable urban development.

Amarilo projects: housing for different life stages

Amarilo develops projects classified as VIS (Social Interest Housing), VIP (Priority Interest Housing) and No VIS. Learn about the differences and some representative examples.

⌂

VIS and VIP housing

Ideal for those seeking an affordable option and access to housing subsidies. Amarilo has developments such as Llano Alto 4 in Villavicencio and Ciudad Mallorquín in Barranquilla, where you can access mortgage loans with state support .

▦

No VIS housing

For those seeking more finishes, larger areas or privileged locations. Examples include Reserva del Dorado or Paseo del Parque in Bogotá, also financeable through mortgage loans .

⌖

Large urban developments

Amarilo participates in megaprojects such as Lagos de Torca in Bogotá, where it develops more than 20,000 homes, and Ciudad Verde in Soacha, conceived as "cities within the city" .

Educational note: This guide does not promote or sell properties. For official information about prices, availability and purchase processes, consult Amarilo's official website directly. The projects mentioned are public examples for educational purposes.

Academia de Soñadores: free financial education

Academia de Soñadores is a platform created by Amarilo in 2019 to help people understand financial topics related to buying a home .

01

Financial security

Covers fundamental concepts such as saving, debt management, cash flow, subsidies and loans. The goal is for you to manage your finances more efficiently .

02

Financial intelligence

Teaches about safe-haven assets, the proper use of severance and bonuses, investments and tax benefits to make smart financial decisions .

03

Wealth

Addresses the importance of controlled expenses, profitability, appreciation and care of acquired assets to maintain and increase their value over time .

Important: Academia de Soñadores is an Amarilo program. This educational guide summarizes its content independently. To access official courses and materials, visit Amarilo's website.

Frequently asked questions about Amarilo

Brief answers based on public information. Details may vary by project and location.

What types of housing does Amarilo offer?

Amarilo classifies its projects as VIS (Social Interest Housing), VIP (Priority Interest Housing) and No VIS. VIS and VIP projects are often eligible for government subsidies, while No VIS projects are aimed at buyers seeking larger areas or premium finishes .

What is Amarilo's Academia de Soñadores?

It is a free financial education platform created by Amarilo in 2019. It covers topics such as saving, mortgage loans, subsidies, investment and wealth management, with the goal of helping people make informed decisions about buying a home .

Does this guide recommend buying from Amarilo?

No. This guide is educational and independent. It does not verify specific projects, inspect properties or offer personalized investment recommendations. Before making a purchase decision, verify the information directly with Amarilo and consult professional advice if necessary.
